# Experiment Protocol v0.1

For every pilot:

## Hypothesis
What must be true for the product to deserve continued investment?

## User
Who experiences the pain and who pays?

## Input
What real artifact/event enters the system?

## Mechanism
What is the smallest transformation we can perform?

## Output
What concrete artifact/state change does the user receive?

## Distribution
Where does the first real user come from?

## Payment
What constitutes a real buying signal?

## Metrics
Track at minimum:
- qualified visitors / prospects
- activation
- time-to-value
- successful outputs
- payment conversion
- repeat use
- support burden
- contribution margin

## Kill criteria
Define before or at the start of the test.
Do not retroactively loosen them to save a favored idea.

## Evidence record
Record:
- inputs
- environment/version
- run identifier
- outputs/artifacts
- failures
- negative evidence
- decision
- next discriminating test
