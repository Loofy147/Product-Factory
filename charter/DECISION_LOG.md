# Decision Log

## D-001 — Portfolio over single-product search
**Date:** 2026-10-06

Decision: build a portfolio of micro-products rather than spend extended cycles searching for one perfect idea.

Rationale: early market research is for hypothesis generation. Real user behavior and payment are more discriminating than additional desk research once a testable product can be shipped.
