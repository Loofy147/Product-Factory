# Pilot Batch 01 — Portfolio Selection v0.1

## Objective

Test three different product-source economics in parallel:

1. BUILD
2. BUY / RESELL
3. WHITE-LABEL / LICENSE or BUNDLE

The purpose is not to maximize code output. It is to compare:
- speed to market
- willingness to pay
- margin
- distribution
- support burden
- legal/contractual friction
- repeatability

## Pilot A — Owned Product

Candidate:
Submission Preflight

Source:
BUILD

Primary value:
Prevent submission failure by checking requirements, evidence, consistency and readiness before final submission.

Initial distribution:
Direct web + targeted B2B outreach

Commercial unit:
Per submission first; subscription only after repeat demand is observed.

Primary kill test:
Real document packages do not produce enough accurate, actionable findings to justify payment.

## Pilot B — Resale Product

Source:
BUY / RESELL

Selection criteria:
- explicit resale/partner rights
- supplier invoices/records
- clear end-user entitlement
- digital fulfillment
- low customer support burden
- positive gross margin after channel fees
- at least two viable distribution channels
- no dependency on one geographic market unless intentional

Candidate families to investigate:
- eSIM / travel connectivity
- digital utility subscriptions
- business software licenses
- digital services with partner/reseller programs

No supplier is approved without documentation of resale rights and unit economics.

## Pilot C — Licensed / Composed Product

Source:
WHITE-LABEL / LICENSE or BUNDLE / COMPOSE

Selection criteria:
- third-party capability can be legitimately packaged
- branding/redistribution rights are explicit
- underlying service is reliable
- our layer creates a distinct customer outcome
- support boundary is explicit
- economics survive a channel fee

Candidate families to investigate:
- specialized AI utilities
- document/automation capability
- business workflow utility
- data transformation capability

## Common distribution matrix

Each pilot must test at least two channels where practical.

Channel classes:
- direct web
- marketplace
- affiliate/referral
- agency/reseller
- B2B direct
- API/developer
- plugin/extension ecosystem
- white-label/partner
- relevant niche marketplaces

## Common evidence record

For every pilot:
- supplier/product source
- rights/terms
- target buyer
- channel
- offer/SKU
- cost
- selling price
- gross margin
- fulfillment path
- support burden
- legal/compliance notes
- first real users
- payment evidence
- repeat behavior
- kill/continue decision

## Immediate research boundary

Research only what is required to source or launch the three pilots.

Do not perform another broad market survey before testing real offers.
