# Pilot B — Travel eSIM Resale

**Source model:** BUY / RESELL  
**Candidate supplier:** Airalo Partners  
**Status:** CANDIDATE — commercial verification required

## Why this candidate

Airalo Partners publicly offers:
- Reseller Platform
- Partner API
- Co-branding
- Affiliate program

The reseller platform supports 900+ eSIM packages across 200+ destinations, lets resellers set their own prices, and distributes eSIMs to customers through Airalo's fulfillment flow.

The Partner API also provides voucher endpoints that allow partners to purchase activation codes individually or in bulk, store them as inventory, and distribute them through their own channels.

## Proposed product

Do not sell "Airalo" as the whole product.

Create focused offers such as:
- Europe travel eSIM
- GCC travel eSIM
- global frequent-traveler eSIM
- destination-specific short-trip bundles

Commercial unit:
one eSIM / voucher.

## Kill test

Kill this candidate if:
- partner terms do not permit our intended channels
- margin after real costs is too thin
- customer support burden is high
- fulfillment reliability is poor
- geographic/activation restrictions prevent repeatable sales
