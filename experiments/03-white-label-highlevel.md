# Pilot C — White-label Service Business OS

**Source model:** WHITE-LABEL / LICENSE  
**Candidate platform:** HighLevel  
**Status:** CANDIDATE — commercial verification required

## Product thesis

Do not resell a generic CRM.

Package a narrow outcome for service businesses:

**Lead capture → follow-up → booking → pipeline → review request**

The underlying platform is white-labeled; our commercial offer is the product.

## Candidate customer

Small service businesses that lose leads because follow-up and booking are inconsistent.

Possible niches:
- home services
- agencies
- beauty / personal services
- local professional services

Choose one niche for the first pilot.

## Proposed offer

Starter:
- branded landing/lead form
- lead pipeline
- automated follow-up
- appointment booking
- review-request workflow

Optional add-ons:
- website
- AI conversation layer
- domain services
- additional automation

## Evidence from vendor

Current official material lists:

HighLevel also documents white-label configurations and SaaS sub-account pricing/markup mechanisms.

## Distribution

- local-business partnerships

## Unit economics

Minimum model:

Vendor plan
+
usage/add-on costs
+
support/onboarding cost
=
monthly cost per customer

Customer price
-
monthly cost
=
gross contribution

Do not use historical vendor prices without re-checking the current account pricing before purchase.

## Kill test

Kill or narrow if:
- customer acquisition cost overwhelms contribution margin
- onboarding/support consumes too much time
- niche customers do not perceive the packaged outcome as materially simpler
- HighLevel restrictions prevent our intended branding/distribution
- churn is high

## Strategic value

This pilot tests whether we can turn an existing platform into a vertical product instead of competing with the platform itself.
